# Forest Apothecary: design guide

Dark, cute, old-style and a little witchy. Calm and never busy: she has abandoned other apps for being too busy.

## Palette
- Forest night (background) #1A241D
- Moss (cards) #26352A, card border #4A5A44
- Parchment (text) #E9DFC7, muted text #B8AE95
- Candle gold (accent, moon) #D8B76E, moon highlight #E6CB86
- Toadstool red (flare button) #9C4A3A
- Potion bottle colours: amber #B5873F, sage #7E8F5E, toadstool #9C4A3A, dusk blue #6B7C8F

## Type
- Headings and medicine names: IM Fell English (Google Fonts), fallback Georgia, serif
- Body and buttons: Karla (Google Fonts), fallback system sans-serif
- Bundle the fonts locally so the app works offline.

## Signature elements
- Each medicine is a little corked potion bottle with a vintage label.
- Live moon in the header, drawn as seen from the southern hemisphere (waxing moon lit on the left), with "N nights to full" under it.
- Daily note card with a dashed border and a small gold star: one short, cute line for the moon phase, plus the next Wheel of the Year date (southern hemisphere: Beltane about 1 November, Litha about 21 December, Lughnasadh about 1 February, Mabon about 21 March, Samhain about 1 May, Yule about 21 June, Imbolc about 1 August, Ostara about 21 September).
- Fine-line ferns and two tiny mushrooms along the bottom edge of the Today screen.
- Gentle names: "Today's pages", "Evening reflection", reminder text "Time for your pages".

## Rules
- Today screen: at most four medicines and two main buttons before scrolling; more medicines behind a "More" bottle.
- Touch targets at least 44px. Text contrast at least 4.5:1.
- No emoji, no gradients, no busy dashboards. One idea per screen.
- Moon content never implies a link to her symptoms. The flares and moon chart sits under "Just for fun".
